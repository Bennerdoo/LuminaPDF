Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

At Lumina PDF, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our local PDF processing application.

1. Local Processing

Lumina PDF is designed as a standalone, private instance. Most PDF processing operations occur locally on your machine or within your private infrastructure. We do not upload your PDF files to external servers for processing unless explicitly configured otherwise.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through this application. Any configuration data or processing history remains within your local environment.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to ensure that your files and data are handled securely within the application's environment.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact your system administrator.
